# Stephane Le Goff's analogue electronics portfolio

Welcome to Dr Le Goff's collection of writings on analogue electronics, with a focus on key concepts such as semiconductor devices (diodes, BJTs, and FETs) and amplifier design. This material is highly suitable for undergraduate students in electrical and electronic engineering or for any engineer/technician wishing to start designing amplifiers.

Click on any link to view/download the corresponding document.
