# Analogue Electronics

Welcome to Stephane Le Goff's collection of writings on analogue electronics, with a focus on key concepts such as semiconductor devices (diodes, BJTs, and FETs) and amplifier design. This material is highly suitable for undergraduate students in electrical and electronic engineering or for any engineer/technician wishing to start designing amplifiers.
